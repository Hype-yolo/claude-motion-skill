# 装回音乐盒齿轮

Local MIT implementation; Three.js MIT renderer

本地编写的解析两连杆 IK、夹爪闭合、抓取携带和释放；不是动物动作库，不是碰撞仿真。

Two-link IK is implemented locally from geometry; no mocap or third-party motion model is imported.

Three.js is supplied by create-scene from installed 0.170.0 with LICENSE where needed. score.js reuses the local neutral score. These components do not imply author/model provenance for the new film.
