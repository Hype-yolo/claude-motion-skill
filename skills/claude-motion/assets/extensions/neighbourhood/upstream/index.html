Wander

Help · E
Click a golden request or pick one from Today’s list. Walk there and try the puzzle, or choose Work with Claude. Watch the tools do the work. Completed favors have a Watch again button.
Be friendly
Tap a neighbor to say hello. Double-tap for a head scratch. Drag them gently, then put them down. Q sends a wave.
Explore
Drag empty space to pan. Scroll or use + / − to zoom. F follows Claude. Home shows the whole neighborhood.
Little extras
T for evening, R for rain, P to pause. Try the pond and flowers. Finish all five favors for the evening gathering.
Little sounds · M
Enable sound with the speaker button. Chalk, keys, paper, pipes and rain are made by the browser. Sound starts off.
Tokens · B
Neighbors hand Claude a stack of Tokens after each favor. Open his satchel to share biscuits or give the town a wind chime and picnic blanket. Each favor pays once per day.
Play · G
Join a ball game, hopscotch, or paper planes. Walking or choosing a favor ends your turn. Games are free.
Tomorrow
Your completed work stays saved in this browser. After five favors, Today’s list lets you begin another day.
