## Made for this project

- The neon and lightbox sign atlas, typeset in the browser by `scripts/bake/`.
- The sixteen advertising campaigns on the LED screens. They are fictional brands drawn at load by `src/city/adscreens.js`.
- All sound and music, synthesised at runtime by `src/audio/audio.js`.
- The KITE's hover pods and lights, the Zenith tower, and the procedural city, facades, rain, fog and clouds.

## Inspiration

[threejspunk.com](https://threejspunk.com) showed how far a cyberpunk city in three.js could go. Neon Zenith started as an attempt to build on that idea, from scratch.
